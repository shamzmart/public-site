Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

At SHAMZ Mart, customer satisfaction is important to us. This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains the circumstances under which orders may be cancelled, products may be returned, refunds may be issued, and promotional benefits such as wallet credits or cashback may be adjusted.

By placing an order through SHAMZ Mart, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to this Refund & Cancellation Policy together with our Terms & Conditions and other applicable policies.

1. Order Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation of an order before it has been packed, processed, dispatched, or otherwise prepared for shipment.

Cancellation requests submitted after shipment may not be accepted unless required by applicable law or specifically approved by SHAMZ Mart.

SHAMZ Mart reserves the right to cancel any order where:

  • Product availability changes.
  • Pricing or inventory errors occur.
  • Fraudulent or suspicious activity is detected.
  • Payment verification fails.
  • Delivery is not possible.
  • The order violates our Terms & Conditions.

2. Return Eligibility

Products may be eligible for return where permitted under applicable law and our policies.

To qualify for a return, products should generally:

  • Be returned within the applicable return period.
  • Be unused unless the return relates to manufacturing defects.
  • Be in substantially the same condition in which they were received.
  • Include original packaging where reasonably available.
  • Include accessories, manuals, warranty cards, invoices, and promotional items supplied with the product where applicable.

Certain products may not be eligible for return due to hygiene, safety, customization, digital delivery, legal restrictions, or other operational reasons.

3. Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be approved in circumstances including, but not limited to:

  • Order cancellation before shipment.
  • Product unavailability.
  • Duplicate payment.
  • Incorrect debit caused by payment failure.
  • Verified damaged or defective products.
  • Incorrect product delivered.
  • Missing products.
  • Refunds required under applicable law.

Refund approval remains subject to verification by SHAMZ Mart.

4. Situations Where Refunds May Not Be Available

  • Products damaged after delivery due to misuse or negligence.
  • Normal wear and tear.
  • Products intentionally damaged.
  • Missing accessories or components.
  • Products returned outside the permitted return period.
  • Incorrect address supplied by the customer.
  • Failure to cooperate during delivery.
  • Violation of these policies.
  • Fraudulent refund requests.

5. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are generally processed through the original payment method. Where this is not reasonably possible, SHAMZ Mart may issue the refund through another suitable payment method, including the SHAMZ Mart Wallet where appropriate.

Banks and payment providers may require additional time before refunded amounts become visible in customer accounts.

Refund processing typically takes between 5–10 business days, although actual timelines may vary depending on the payment provider, banking institution, public holidays, and regulatory requirements.

6. Partial Refunds

Where only part of an order is returned or where permitted by applicable law, SHAMZ Mart may issue a partial refund.

Refund amounts may be adjusted for:

  • Missing accessories.
  • Missing promotional items.
  • Damaged packaging.
  • Partial order cancellations.
  • Any deductions permitted under applicable law.

7. Cash on Delivery (COD)

For eligible Cash on Delivery orders:

  • Payment is collected upon delivery.
  • Approved refunds may be issued through bank transfer, UPI, wallet credit, or another suitable payment method.
  • Refusing delivery repeatedly without valid reason may affect future COD eligibility.

8. Wallet Refunds

Where permitted, refunds may be credited to the SHAMZ Mart Wallet instead of the original payment method. Wallet refunds may be used for future purchases through the Platform in accordance with the Wallet Policy.

Wallet balances:

  • Cannot normally be exchanged for cash unless required by applicable law.
  • Cannot be transferred between customer accounts.
  • May be restricted in cases of fraud or misuse.
  • Remain subject to the Wallet Policy.

9. Cashback & Reward Reversal

Orders eligible for cashback or promotional rewards remain subject to verification and compliance with the applicable Reward Policy.

SHAMZ Mart reserves the right to reverse, cancel, or adjust cashback where:

  • The order is cancelled.
  • The order is refunded.
  • The order is returned.
  • Payment is reversed or charged back.
  • Fraudulent activity is detected.
  • Cashback was credited due to a technical or operational error.
  • The customer becomes ineligible under the applicable reward campaign.

If cashback has already been credited and subsequently becomes ineligible, SHAMZ Mart may deduct the corresponding amount from the customer's wallet, future cashback credits, or other eligible refunds where permitted by applicable law.

Reward eligibility, calculation, installment schedules, expiry, and campaign conditions are governed by the applicable Reward Policy and Terms & Conditions.

10. Replacement Policy

Where appropriate, SHAMZ Mart may offer a replacement instead of a refund.

Replacement requests remain subject to:

  • Verification of the reported issue.
  • Availability of replacement stock.
  • Compliance with applicable return conditions.

If replacement is not reasonably possible, SHAMZ Mart may instead provide a refund in accordance with this Policy.

11. Layaway Purchases

SHAMZ Mart may offer Layaway for selected products, allowing customers to pay for eligible products over multiple installments before delivery.

  • Products remain reserved while the layaway plan remains active.
  • Ownership of the product remains with SHAMZ Mart until full payment has been received.
  • Products will not be delivered until the purchase price has been paid in full.
  • Customers may request cancellation of an active layaway plan subject to applicable policies.
  • Refunds for cancelled layaway plans may be subject to deductions or cancellation charges where permitted.
  • Missed payments may result in cancellation of the layaway plan.
  • Layaway is not a loan, financing facility, or banking product.

12. EMI Purchases (Future Feature)

SHAMZ Mart may introduce EMI or installment purchase options in the future.

Where available, EMI purchases shall be governed by separate eligibility requirements, lender policies, and additional terms presented during checkout.

13. Failed or Duplicate Payments

Customers should promptly notify SHAMZ Mart if:

  • A payment fails after funds have been debited.
  • The same transaction is charged multiple times.
  • An incorrect amount has been charged.

After verification, eligible duplicate or incorrect payments will be refunded in accordance with this Policy.

14. Inspection Process

Returned products may be inspected by SHAMZ Mart before a refund or replacement is approved.

Inspection may include verification of:

  • Product condition.
  • Reported defects.
  • Packaging.
  • Accessories.
  • Serial numbers.
  • Warranty labels.

Receipt of a returned product does not automatically guarantee approval of a refund.

15. Return Shipping

Where a return has been approved, SHAMZ Mart will provide instructions for the return process.

Depending on the reason for return:

  • SHAMZ Mart may bear the return shipping cost.
  • The customer may be responsible for return shipping costs where permitted by applicable law.

16. Fraud Prevention

To protect customers and the Platform, SHAMZ Mart reserves the right to deny, delay, or investigate refund requests where fraud or abuse is reasonably suspected.

Examples include:

  • Repeated abusive refund requests.
  • False damage claims.
  • Return fraud.
  • Chargeback abuse.
  • Identity verification failures.
  • Promotion or cashback abuse.

Accounts involved in fraudulent activities may be suspended or permanently terminated.

17. Force Majeure

Refunds, returns, deliveries, and replacements may be delayed due to events beyond SHAMZ Mart's reasonable control, including natural disasters, public health emergencies, strikes, transportation disruptions, governmental restrictions, internet outages, payment network failures, or other force majeure events.

18. Incorrect Refunds

If a refund or wallet credit is issued incorrectly due to a technical, operational, or human error, SHAMZ Mart reserves the right to recover or reverse the incorrectly refunded amount in accordance with applicable law.

19. Dispute Resolution

If you believe a refund request has been incorrectly rejected or processed, please contact SHAMZ Mart before initiating any payment dispute or chargeback. We will make reasonable efforts to resolve concerns fairly and promptly.

Nothing in this Policy limits any rights available to consumers under applicable law.

20. Changes to this Refund & Cancellation Policy

SHAMZ Mart may revise this Refund & Cancellation Policy from time to time to reflect changes in business practices, payment methods, legal requirements, Platform features, or operational procedures.

The latest version of this Policy will always be published through our website and mobile application. Continued use of the Platform after the updated Policy becomes effective constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
